Alarms

    Settings

    Alarm sound

    Preview uses this tap to unlock iPad audio. The real alarm is much longer and loops until the pushups are done.

    Snooze

    Snooze only pauses the noise. The pushups are still required when it rings again.

    Night brightness

    This darkens the clock after you arm the alarm. It does not change the iPad’s system brightness. The pushup screen stays bright.

    Camera

    Frames are processed on this iPad. Nothing is uploaded. If you tapped Don’t Allow, go to Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow, then on the site tap aA → Website Settings → Camera → Allow, and reload.

    Emergency override

    Off by default. Turn this on only if the camera cannot work and you still need a way to silence a ringing alarm. You must press and hold for 4 seconds. It is not shown as a skip when the camera is merely asking for permission.

    Pushup Alarm stores alarms on this iPad only. Camera video never leaves the device.

    Camera is blocked

    Pushups can only be counted with the front camera. This screen has no skip button.

    1. Open the iPad Settings app.
    2. Tap Safari, then Camera, and choose Ask or Allow.
    3. Open this page in Safari, tap aA in the address bar, tap Website Settings, and set Camera to Allow.
    4. Come back here and tap Try again. If you added the app to the Home Screen, you can also open the site in Safari once to change the permission.
